Features

Built for dancers who want to get better

Pose Detection

Frame-by-frame skeletal tracking maps every joint in your body. Overlay it on your video to see exactly how you move.

Scorecards

Rate movement, musicality, footwork, and styling on custom scorecards. Track your trend lines to see real progress.

Weight Shift & Timing

Visualise where your weight sits on every beat. See speed graphs and body mechanics that you can't catch in a mirror.

Body Focus

Isolate upper body, lower body, or full body to drill into the details that matter for each move.
