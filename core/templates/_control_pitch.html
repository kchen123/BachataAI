The Problem

👥

Group Classes Don't Work

20+ students, one instructor. You get maybe 30 seconds of individual feedback per hour. Everyone learns the same choreography regardless of level.

💸

Privates Are Expensive

$80-150/hr for one-on-one coaching. Most dancers can only afford 1-2 sessions per month. Progress stalls between lessons.

🚫

No Practice Tools

Between classes, dancers have zero structured way to practice. No drills, no feedback, no tracking. You just wait until next week.

🧑‍🎤

Leads Can't Improve

Leading is about subtle body mechanics — frame, weight shifts, micro-signals. You can't see these in a mirror. You need frame-by-frame analysis to actually improve.
